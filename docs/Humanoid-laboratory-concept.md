# Neira: humanoid and laboratory concept

The owner changed the direction to a spacious, expressive two-storey laboratory, approximately 300 square metres per floor, and a humanoid Neira with legs and feminine forms. The previous compact wheeled workcell is historical, not a constraint.

## Current concept
Neira is a nominal 1700 mm robot with two legs, knees, ankles and feet; a narrow waist, wider hips, shaped torso, two arms, simple hands and an optical visor. Pearl protective envelopes and graphite joint envelopes remain native editable spline parts. These are solid appearance/packaging studies, not hollow manufactured skins or selected actuators.
